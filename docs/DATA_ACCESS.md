# Academic dataset access

Version: 20261017-v2. This is a document-based procedure for the compact dataset, not an automated access service. Preparing these documents does not record anyone's acceptance or grant new access. Repository visibility is unchanged.

## For a researcher

1. Read the full [Matterport Academic Use Agreement](https://matterport.com/legal/matterport-end-user-license-agreement-academic-use-model-data) and [IDEN-VLN project terms](../dataset/TERMS_OF_USE.md).
2. Complete the [acceptance form](DATA_ACCESS_AGREEMENT.md) through a private channel agreed with the repository owner. Do not post names, email addresses, signed documents, or research access credentials in public GitHub issues. A public issue may ask how to establish private contact, without including personal details.
3. Receive the owner's countersigned acceptance and access to the identified compact dataset version. Signing a template alone does not grant repository access. Each additional recipient needs a separate acceptance record.

## Owner procedure before providing access

- Record the intended recipient, academic purpose, exact dataset Git commit and checksum-manifest SHA256. Identify the individual who will receive access; a signature on behalf of an institution requires authority to represent it.
- Supply the full provider agreement, or its link and a preserved dated copy, plus the exact project terms. Use the form to obtain affirmative acceptance of both before delivery. Apply this procedure to every new dataset recipient rather than guessing whether a distribution is a substantial portion.
- Countersign and retain the executed document privately, with timestamps, copies/hashes of the accepted terms, and delivery details. Store records outside Git. `/access-records/` is ignored as an additional safeguard, not a security boundary; never force-add records. Restrict access and handle retention/disclosure under applicable privacy law.
- Follow Matterport section 2.4(d): make the required reasonable efforts, to the extent permitted by law, to collect recipient name, email and academic institution, obtain consent to share those details with Matterport for its internal/outreach purposes, and promptly provide collected information through an appropriate private provider channel. Record that step or a lawful reason it cannot be completed. The form makes the intended disclosure explicit; no information is collected or sent automatically by this repository.
- Only after completed acceptance, provide a compact **dataset-only** package containing records, split files, data card, checksums, notice, project terms and the provider agreement link. Do not use a full-repository invitation as a shortcut: unresolved inherited-code/media rights are separate, and repository access exposes more than this dataset permission covers.
- Record the delivery time and package identity; verify its checksums. Do not make the repository or dataset publicly downloadable as part of this procedure. Existing access has not been audited or retroactively accepted by this change.

## Scope and remaining limits

The owner-controlled code grant remains MIT. Dataset terms grant only rights the owner controls and retain provider conditions. These documents implement an administrative path; they do not determine legal ownership of every field, authorize commercial reuse, or establish blanket clearance of the whole repository. Broader public distribution or a Creative Commons release needs its own rights review. No third party has been contacted by this update.
